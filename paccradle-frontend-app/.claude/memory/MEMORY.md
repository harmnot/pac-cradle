# App Context — Paccradle (Photo-to-Cardboard Box Inner Insert Generator)

## 1. Product Overview & Core Value Proposition
**paccradle** is a single-sided, self-serve B2B/Prosumer micro-SaaS web application. It converts a smartphone photo of physical products resting on a standard sheet of paper (A4 or US Letter) into a **100% production-ready, custom-contoured cardboard box inner insert dieline** (`.DXF`, `.SVG`, and 1:1 `.PDF`).

* **The Market Gap:** Existing packaging tools (Pacdora, DieCutTemplates, GoVisually) only generate standard rectangular *outer* boxes or generic square partitions. None of them extract irregular 2D silhouettes of real physical objects (perfume bottles, skincare droppers, electronics, artisanal jars) from a photo and embed them into a foldable **FEFCO 09-Series Crown/Tray Inner Insert** with automatic finger-pull notches and cardboard thickness tolerances.
* **Business Model:** Zero sellers, zero cold outreach, zero subscription fatigue. **Preview-First, Pay-Per-Export ($X/download or $XX for XX credits)** via Merchant of Record (Lemon Squeezy / Paddle) or QRIS/VA. Users inspect the interactive 2D dieline and 3D folded tray in the browser before paying, reducing chargeback risk to near zero.

---

## 2. Target Users & High-Intent Search Entry Points
1. **E-Commerce & Indie Brands (Shopify / Etsy / Cosmetics / Hampers):** Replacing cheap styrofoam with eco-friendly folded paperboard/corrugated inserts for unboxing experiences.
2. **Local Packaging & Print Shops:** Need a fast way to generate custom insert dielines for clients without spending 2 hours manually tracing bottles in Adobe Illustrator or ArtiosCAD.
3. **Laser-Cutting & Maker Shops (xTool / Glowforge / Cricut / CNC Drag-Knife):** Need clean, closed-path `.DXF`/`.SVG` files to cut short-run packaging prototypes.
